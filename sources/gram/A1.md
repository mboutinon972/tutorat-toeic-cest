## 1. Le verbe *to be* (être)

| Pronom | Affirmatif | Forme courte | Négatif | Question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I | I am | I'm | I am not (I'm not) | Am I ...? |
| you | you are | you're | you are not (aren't) | Are you ...? |
| he / she / it | he is | he's | is not (isn't) | Is he ...? |
| we | we are | we're | are not (aren't) | Are we ...? |
| they | they are | they're | are not (aren't) | Are they ...? |

**Exemples :** *She is a manager. We are not late. Is Mr. Kim in the office?*

**Attention :** *I have 30 years* est faux. On dit *I am 30* (l'âge se dit avec *to be*). De même : *I am hot / cold / hungry*.

## 2. Le verbe *to have* (avoir)

| Pronom | Affirmatif | Négatif | Question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | have | do not (don't) have | Do you have ...? |
| he / she / it | **has** | does not (doesn't) have | Does she have ...? |

**Exemples :** *We have a meeting at 9. My boss has a new car. Does he have a laptop?*

## 3. Le présent simple (habitudes et vérités générales)

Verbe à la forme de base. À **he / she / it**, on ajoute **-s**.

| Pronom | work | study | go | watch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I / you / we / they | work | study | go | watch |
| he / she / it | work**s** | stud**ies** | go**es** | watch**es** |

**Règles du -s :** la plupart des verbes + **-s** ; verbes en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o + **-es** ; consonne + y devient **-ies** (study, studies) ; *have* devient **has**.

| Forme | Structure | Exemple |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Affirmatif | sujet + verbe(-s) | He drinks coffee at 8 a.m. |
| Négatif | sujet + do / does + **not** + verbe de base | They do not work on Sunday. |
| Question | Do / Does + sujet + verbe de base ? | Does Mr. Kim speak French? |

**Piège :** après *does*, le verbe n'a **pas** de -s (*Does she work?* et non *Does she works?*).

**Adverbes de fréquence** (placés avant le verbe, après *to be*) : always, usually, often, sometimes, never. *I always send an email. She is never late.*

## 4. Le présent continu (action en cours)

**be + verbe-ing** : *I am writing an email. They are not working. Is he driving?*

| Présent simple | Présent continu |
| :--- | :--- |
| habitude : *I work in Paris.* | maintenant : *I am working now.* |

## 5. Articles et pluriels

| Cas | Règle | Exemples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **a** | devant un son consonne | a desk, a manager |
| **an** | devant un son voyelle | an email, an office |
| **the** | chose précise ou déjà connue | The meeting is on Monday. |
| Pluriel simple | + **s** | desks, chairs |
| Pluriel en -s, -sh, -ch, -x | + **es** | boxes, watches |
| Consonne + y | **-ies** | company, companies |
| Pluriels irréguliers | à apprendre | man, men ; woman, women ; child, children ; person, people |

**This / that / these / those :** *this desk* (près, singulier), *that door* (loin), *these files* (près, pluriel), *those chairs* (loin, pluriel).

## 6. *There is / There are*, *some / any*

- *There is a printer in the room.* (singulier) — *There are ten chairs.* (pluriel)
- Négation : *There isn't / aren't*. Question : *Is there ...? Are there ...?*
- **some** en phrase affirmative : *There are some files.* — **any** en négation et question : *There aren't any files. Are there any files?*

## 7. Prépositions de temps et de lieu

| Préposition | Temps | Lieu |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **at** | une heure précise : *at 9 o'clock, at noon* | un point : *at the desk, at the door* |
| **on** | un jour : *on Monday, on May 1* | une surface, un étage : *on the table, on the second floor* |
| **in** | mois, année, saison, partie du jour : *in August, in 2026, in the morning* | un espace, une ville : *in the office, in Lyon* |

**Autres :** *from ... to* (de ... à), *between ... and*, *next to* (à côté de), *near* (près de), *behind*, *in front of*.

## 8. Les mots interrogatifs

| Mot | Sens | Exemple |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Who | qui | Who is the new manager? |
| What | quoi, quel | What is your name? |
| Where | où | Where is the meeting room? |
| When / What time | quand / à quelle heure | When do you leave? |
| Why | pourquoi (réponse : *because*) | Why are you happy? |
| How | comment | How do you go to work? |
| How much | combien (indénombrable, prix) | How much is the ticket? |
| How many | combien (dénombrable) | How many computers are there? |
| How old / long / often | quel âge / combien de temps / à quelle fréquence | How often do you travel? |

## 9. Nombres, heures, dates

| Nombres | |
| :--- | :--- |
| 13 thirteen, 14 fourteen ... 19 nineteen | **-teen** (accent sur la fin) |
| 30 thirty, 40 forty, 50 fifty ... 90 ninety | **-ty** (accent au début) |

**Heures :** 3:00 *three o'clock* ; 3:15 *a quarter past three* ; 3:30 *half past three* ; 3:45 *a quarter to four* ; 12:00 *noon*.

**Dates :** on utilise les **ordinaux** : *first, second, third, fourth ... twentieth*. *The meeting is on May 2nd* (lu *May the second*).

## 10. Impératif et *can*

- **Impératif :** verbe de base. *Please open the window.* Négatif : *Don't close the door.*
- ***can* (pouvoir, savoir)** : *I can speak English.* Négatif : *cannot / can't*. Question : *Can you help me?* Pas de -s à la 3e personne.

## 11. Pièges fréquents pour les francophones

| Erreur | Correct |
| :--- | :--- |
| I have 25 years | I am 25 |
| She work here | She work**s** here |
| Does he works? | Does he work? |
| I am agree | I agree |
| The informations | The information (indénombrable) |
| Actually = en réalité (≠ actuellement) | *Currently / at the moment* = actuellement |
